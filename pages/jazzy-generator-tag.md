Substitutes the standard WordPress generator tag with a more creative version. Rather than displaying plain text like "WordPress 4.5," it shows something like "WordPress 4.5 to the sounds of Coleman Hawkins."

## Download

Get the latest release (version 6.6.0) from WordPress.org:
[jazzy-generator-tag.6.6.0.zip](https://downloads.wordpress.org/plugin/jazzy-generator-tag.6.6.0.zip)
