If you've ever written a post using the `link` post format, you know that the experience leaves a little something to be desired in most themes.

Redirect Link Format adds a little magic to how your visitors interact with these posts by simply redirecting to the linked URL when someone visits the post. That's it.

The redirect is only triggered on published and scheduled posts of any type where the post format is set to `link`.
