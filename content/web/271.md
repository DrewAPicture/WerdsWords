Gmail has apparently retired several Labs features including my favorite (and most used), Mouse Gestures. In a recent [post](http://gmailblog.blogspot.com/2012/05/say-hello-or-ola-or-halo-or-salam-to.html) on the Gmail blog, other retired Labs features include Old Snakey, Mail Goggles, Hiding Unread Counts, Custom Date Formats and some others. Bummer and a half!
