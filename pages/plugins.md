## [Jazzy Generator Tag](/jazzy-generator-tag/)

Supplements your WordPress site's generator tag with the jazz artist your current version of WordPress was named after.

## [Press This New Post](/press-this-new-post/)

Adds a "Press This" item to the "+ New" drop-down in the toolbar for directly accessing the Press This posting form.

## [Redirect Link Format](/redirect-link-format/)

Auto-redirects link format posts to a URL specified in the content area.

## [Remove Dashboard Access](/remove-dashboard-access-for-non-admins/)

Disables dashboard access for users of a specific role or capability. Disallowed users are redirected to a chosen URL. *Acquired October 2024.*

## [Support Me](/support-me/)

Generates expirable user accounts for support purposes. *Acquired October 2024.*
