Restricts WordPress dashboard access based on user role. Non-administrator users who lack the `manage_options` capability are automatically redirected to the site's homepage.

For a full overview of capabilities and roles, see the [WordPress Roles and Capabilities documentation](http://codex.wordpress.org/Roles_and_Capabilities).

**Note:** This plugin was acquired in October 2024 and is no longer maintained by me.

## Download

Available from the [WordPress plugin repository](http://wordpress.org/extend/plugins/remove-dashboard-access-for-non-admins/).
